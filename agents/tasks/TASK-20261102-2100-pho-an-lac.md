# Phố An Lạc — Hốt Họ Vỉa Hè

## Wish being fulfilled
Players want the street to feel like a real Vietnamese sidewalk **hụi / hốt họ** night — plastic stools, a battered tin box, numbers that somehow *know* you. Soft IRL fantasy: on Phố An Lạc you sit the circle once, a golden số nổ floats above the asphalt, and the whole phố cheers a luck you cannot buy outside the temple gate.

## Player fantasy loop (addictive)
1. **Discover** — after street warp (FAB / KeyP / ~x 20.2), spot a small **bàn hốt họ** on the south vỉa hè (opposite karaoke north curb): 3–4 ghế nhựa + hộp tôn + sổ giấy + warm floor ring. Soft tip within ~30s: **「Hốt họ」**
2. **Interact** — press interact → short **rút số** (~5–7s): player sits/leans at circle (`S.busy`), numbers spin as floating digit sprites or toast cascade; one số **nổ** with gold spark + stool bob; Esc/Hủy after ≥1.5s still counts as 1 lượt
3. **Reward** — first draw: toast VN + công đức + Sổ line **🎲 Hốt Họ Phố An Lạc**; light VFX (gold dust / ring flash). Soft ting / reuse whoosh
4. **Escalate** — draw count unlocks: (a) after 1 → **Họ đỏ** (warmer glow + bigger nổ), (b) after 3 → **Chủ Họ** flair + optional nearby street-lamp warm pulse (do **not** rewrite karaoke Đêm loa / xích lô đêm), (c) soft daily tip “hốt một tay trên phố” if streak tips exist (do not hard-rewrite streak)
5. **Show off** — flair **Chủ Họ** on merit chip / `templeFlairSuffix`; Sổ section lists lượt / bậc; friends see a street lucky, not only Khách Phố / Ca Sĩ Vỉa Hè / Hành Khách Xích Lô
